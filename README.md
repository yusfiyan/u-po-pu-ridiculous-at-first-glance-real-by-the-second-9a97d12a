## The pitch

> We bottle the silence of empty chapels and sell it to open-plan offices, because frazzled workers will pay to hear nothing — and nothing is the cheapest thing we can record.

One sentence, 173 characters. A bizarre startup that sounds like a joke — selling bottled silence — until the business logic lands: open-plan offices are the named buyer, stressed workers are a real market for quiet, and the product costs almost nothing to produce.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the entry text to `entry.txt` inside the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. It validates the gate before writing: 1–200 characters, not whitespace-only, a single sentence on a single line.

